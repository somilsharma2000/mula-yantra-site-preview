The company

One origin. Many systems.

MULA YANTRA researches, designs, builds, owns and operates independent technology products — from fitness and healthcare to commerce.

Each product is developed as its own system with its own identity, backed by shared foundations: one engineering culture, one design language, one standard of dependability.

The name carries our intent. Mula — the root, the origin point. Yantra — a precise instrument. Together: the foundational instrument through which new possibilities are created.
